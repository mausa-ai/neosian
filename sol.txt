Probability of Drawing 2 Aces in a Row (Without Replacement)
=============================================================

Given: A standard deck of 52 cards containing 4 aces.

Step 1: P(1st card is an Ace) = 4/52
Step 2: P(2nd card is an Ace | 1st was an Ace) = 3/51

Combined Probability:
  P(2 Aces in a row) = (4/52) x (3/51)
                      = 12 / 2652
                      = 1 / 221
                      ≈ 0.004525 (or about 0.4525%)

Answer: 1/221 ≈ 0.4525%
